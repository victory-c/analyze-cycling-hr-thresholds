# Final report contract

Write the report in the user's language. Lead with the recommended anchors and zones, then expose the evidence and uncertainty that justify them.

## Required sections

1. **Executive result** — final VT1, cycling LTHR/LT2, RCP if identifiable, and confidence/ranges.
2. **Garmin audit** — inventory, sensor/power coverage, candidate efforts, method, independent conclusion, and limitations.
3. **CPET audit** — files/columns/units, timing and alignment, protocol, signal quality, independent VT1/RCP conclusions, and high-end validity.
4. **Reconciliation** — why one source is preferred for each construct and why disagreement exists. State explicitly that values were not blindly averaged.
5. **Zones** — model name, physiological anchors, formulas, rounding, bpm ranges, exact percentage-of-LTHR ranges, and confidence inherited from anchors.
6. **Garmin setup** — custom BPM lower boundaries and, if relevant, the device's forced percentage result and mismatch.
7. **Caveats** — what cannot be inferred from the available data.
8. **Best added test** — exactly one test, chosen for maximum information gain.

## Zone table fields

Include zone, bpm lower/upper, exact lower/upper `%LTHR`, physiological domain, intended use, and the threshold that anchors the boundary. Use “open-ended” if HRmax is unknown; do not invent a maximum.

## Language discipline

- Distinguish “estimated,” “observed,” “configured,” and “derived.”
- Use “not identifiable from this source” instead of manufacturing a number.
- Call automatic breakpoint results “candidates” until physiologically adjudicated.
- Avoid medical diagnosis and categorical sensor-failure claims without evidence.
- Do not expose names, exact activity IDs, coordinates, account identifiers, or raw health files.
